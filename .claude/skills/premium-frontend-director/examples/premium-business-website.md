# Example: Czech construction company

## Brief

Create a public site for a Czech construction company using verified services, real project photography, approved company facts, and a contact path. No supplied facts should be invented to make the page feel complete.

## Direction

Use **Persuade** for the company and contact pages, with **Experience** for genuine project storytelling. A structural grid or material detail may become a signature motif only if it supports the real work and brand. Prefer project-specific imagery over generic hard-hat stock.

## Implementation cues

- Build clear service, project, about, and contact routes from verified information.
- Mark missing references, certifications, statistics, and office details as content requests, not production copy.
- Preserve CMS fields, slugs, preview behavior, and form delivery.
- Localize Czech content and test diacritics, form labels, privacy copy, and mobile navigation.

## Acceptance evidence

Check representative project and service pages, the mobile contact path, keyboard navigation, form success/error states, and public SEO metadata. Report any unavailable real asset or unverified fact.
